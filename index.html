Podcast Stock Tracker

This site tracks stocks explicitly pitched on value-investing podcasts — and then checks how each pitch actually did. Every pitch is recorded with the speaker, the episode and the evidence for the call, entered at the first trading close after the episode, and its performance is measured against SPY over the same dates, so a pick only counts as a win if it beat the index. Coverage: four active shows — We Study Billionaires, Value After Hours, The Acquirers Podcast and Business Breakdowns. The core window is 2 Aug – 2 Nov 2026 (42 episodes), and Value After Hours and We Study Billionaires rows have now been resolved from full episode transcripts back to Nov 2025, so those two shows carry additional resolved picks, holds, watchlist entries and passes from the earlier months. A fifth show, Focused Compounding, is paused and is not currently tracked. All returns end on the last completed trading day — currently the 10 Nov 2026 close — and are refreshed daily to the latest completed close.

Methodology — how picks are scored

The rules below are fixed in advance and published with the data, so anyone can check a number — or see exactly why something was left out.

Explicit pitches only

Only stocks a speaker explicitly pitches on the episode are tracked — a clear buy case, a stated position, or (for Business Breakdowns) a full deep-dive thesis. Passing mentions, sponsor reads, and stocks merely discussed with no call made are never picks. Where a show gives its own verdict — a stock added to We Study Billionaires’ Intrinsic Value Portfolio, or not added — that verdict decides Long vs. Pass.

Entry price

The first trading close after the episode date — a listener couldn’t act before hearing the pitch. For Value After Hours the episode date is the YouTube livestream date (Tuesdays), not the Thursday podcast date, so entries use the first close after the livestream.

Benchmark

SPY over the exact same dates as each pick. A pick only “wins” if it beats SPY, not merely if it goes up. Longs and Shorts are always tracked and reported separately.

Return period & refresh

Every return runs from the pick’s entry close to the last completed trading day — currently the 10 Nov 2026 close — and returns are refreshed daily to the latest completed close. No return ever uses a partial, still-open trading day.

No speaker rankings

Speakers are not ranked and there is no leaderboard or winner. Most speakers have only a handful of scored picks, so a ranking would be decided by noise, not record. This page shows unranked counts of picks by speaker instead.

Holds and exits

Holds count as Long, flagged as Hold, and are not scored at the episode-entry price (the position was bought earlier). A sold exit closes the original Long — its performance to the exit date counts — and is never recorded as a new Short.

Watchlist and Pass

Watchlist = liked but not bought, usually on valuation. Pass = discussed but too hard to call. Neither is scored; later price moves are labelled “what happened after”, never a pick return.

Preferreds and ETFs

Tracked, but separately from common-stock picks, and on total return including dividends/distributions — price-only returns would misstate them. Melvin’s preferred names (FUTP, FCNCN, MBIN, PFFA and others) sit in the review list until tickers are confirmed.

Mastermind and guest segments

We Study Billionaires rows from Mastermind panels, guest-fund episodes (for example a guest describing their own fund's holdings) and bull/bear debates are kept in a separate Mastermind / guest list. They are not Intrinsic Value Portfolio decisions and are never scored as IVP Longs, however bullish the segment sounds. Within that list, a row is badged Long where the transcript shows a pick or a holding, and Needs review where it shows no pick decision.

Mentions are not picks

A ticker that is only mentioned — an anecdote, a board seat, a product the speaker issues, or a name that appears only in an episode title — is not a pick. Tickers checked against full Value After Hours transcripts on that basis (GILD, ELMD, BKMG, UFOD, MSTU and TSLA from S08 E11) were removed from the lists entirely. BLDR from S08 E14 is not in that removed group: Bloomstran said the wrong name “builder supply” for the position in the transcript, and BLDR is also tagged in the episode title, so that position is recorded as a BLDR Long on that basis. A stock discussed at length but with no explicit personal buy/own decision in the episode — BRK.B in S08 E14 and S08 E16 — is likewise not scored as a Long. Those two BRK.B rows are kept as resolved no-decision records, labelled “No decision in episode — not scored”, each with its full-transcript link — they are not in Needs review.

Exclusions

PINE is excluded — it is an asset owned by CTO, not a Melvin pick. Passing mentions and sponsor reads are never picks. Nothing is silently deleted; reclassifications are noted on the record.
